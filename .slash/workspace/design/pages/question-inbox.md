# Page Override: Question Inbox

- **Layout**: Full-width list of questions, optimized for mobile vertical scrolling.
- **Differences from System**: 
  - Each item in the list must prominently feature the **Signature Element** (the time-elapsed indicator) on the right side.
  - Unread/Pending questions have a subtle accent-color tint or indicator.
  - Completed questions are muted (lower opacity, darker tint).
